Context Menu

Switch off the options you never use. Everything in Zen's right-click menus is listed here, including what Zen, your other mods and your extensions add. Changes apply the next time you open a menu.

This is sample data. Open this page from Zen (right-click an empty part of the sidebar → Customize Context Menus…) to change your menus.

Not sure where to start? Hide what Zen Context Menu used to hide by default: Bookmark Tab and Bookmark Link, Reload Tab, Select All Tabs, Close Multiple Tabs, Search in a Private Window, Print Selection and Select All.

Something missing? Extensions' items, and a few of Zen's, only exist while their menu is open. Open the menu once and they'll be listed here.
